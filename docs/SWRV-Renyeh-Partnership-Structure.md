# SWRV On The Go × Renyeh — Partnership Structure
*Locked in. Reference this whenever there's a question about who does what.*

---

## The Model

**Renyeh** handles front-end client work — the relationship, the design, the delivery, the social media, her own company services. She brings the clients and manages the experience.

**Swerve / SWRV On The Go** handles backend infrastructure — the technical build, hosting, integrations, systems. No client-facing chaos, no overlap.

**Split:** She pays SWRV half of whatever she charges for the website build.
